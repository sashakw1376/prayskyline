The West Loch area connects nearby residential neighborhoods with the wider communities of ʻEwa and Waipahu. Families, commuters, workers, and students travel through this area every day, each carrying their own responsibilities, hopes, busyness and challenges.

## Pray for:
- Students, teachers, counselors, coaches, and families in West Loch and Waipahu including Honowai Elementary and Kaleiopu’u Elementary. Pray for healthy learning environments where students are engaged and encouraged to learn. Pray against bullying and peer pressure. Pray that those who know Jesus will be His light on their campuses.
- Healing for patients, peace and provision for families, wisdom and clarity for the medical teams, and open doors for the chaplains at Queen’s West.
- Children and teens receiving treatment at Queen’s Medical Center Kahi Mohala, a place that provides behavioral health treatment for children, their families, and caregivers. Ask God for healing and for all to experience the love, hope and peace found only in Jesus.
- New Hope Leeward as they prepare for a Zoe Japan fundraising walk in the spring.
- Churches: Pearlside Ewa and Harbor Church West Oahu
