The airport is a gateway to Hawaiʻi, welcoming visitors, reconnecting families, and sending residents and travelers across the world. Behind every arrival and departure is a story—celebration, adventure, work, family separation, or sometimes grief and uncertainty.

## Pray for:
- Airport employees, travelers, hospitality workers, and everyone arriving in or departing from Hawaiʻi.
- Ask God to bless those who work at the airport and the thousands of people who pass through each day.
- Workers to be filled with patience, kindness, & compassion, even during stressful moments.
- May visitors experience the spirit of aloha through the people they meet. As they take in the beauty of our island home, may they be drawn to the Creator.

<p class="verse">“Do not forget to show hospitality to strangers, for by so doing some people have shown hospitality to angels without knowing it.” Hebrews 13:2</p>
