Pearl Highlands is a busy gathering and shopping area serving Pearl City and surrounding communities. People pass through for work, errands, appointments, and time with family, often moving quickly from one responsibility to the next. Behind the daily activity are workers, parents, students, and older adults with different needs and stories.

## Pray for:
- Large businesses in the area — Home Depot, Sam’s Club, Pearl Highlands, Walmart –- pray for their employees as they serve the community.
- CARES Waiawa (formerly Urban Garden Center) — UH Education and Gardening resource. People growing healthy food. May they thank God for His creation.
- Elderly residents at The Plaza and other nursing facilities, praying they receive compassionate care, meaningful connections, and opportunities to experience Christ’s love through weekly Bible studies and local church outreach.
- Growing number of people experiencing homelessness throughout the Skyline corridor. Ask God to provide for their physical needs and open doors for them to experience the hope, love, and saving grace of Jesus.
- God’s protection and encouragement for students, teachers, and staff at Pearl City High School, Highlands Intermediate, Lehua Elementary, and Pearl City Elementary.

<p class="verse">“The Lord will watch over your coming and going both now and forevermore.” Psalm 121:8</p>
