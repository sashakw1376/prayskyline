The Lagoon Drive and Māpunapuna area is home to many businesses, workplaces, and people whose daily efforts keep services and transportation moving across Oʻahu. Many workers spend long hours providing for their families, often with little recognition for the important work they do. Pray for safe working conditions, fair treatment, and encouragement for employees and business owners. Students and professionals transfer here for the A and U buses to the University of Hawaii at Manoa.

## Pray for:

- People working and living around Mapunapuna, Lagoon Drive and Moanalua—including those who may feel unseen or forgotten.
- Strengthen ministries serving vulnerable people.
- Give Your people compassion and wisdom to respond to practical needs while pointing people toward the hope found in Jesus.
- Schools: Aliamanu Elementary, Nimitz Elementary, Moanalua High School
