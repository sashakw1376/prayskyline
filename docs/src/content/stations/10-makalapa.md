The Makalapa area is closely connected to military service and the families and civilian workers who support it. As of 2022, Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam has a population of over 107,000 military service members, their families, and civilians. Service members and their loved ones may experience demanding schedules, frequent moves, deployments, and time apart from one another.

## Pray for:
- Military members, spouses, children and families who experience deployments, moves and separation.
- Protection for those who serve our country.
- Give strength to spouses and children during times of separation.
- Help churches embrace military families and provide genuine friendship, support and community.
- Schools: Makalapa Elementary, Pearl Harbor Elementary, Radford High School
