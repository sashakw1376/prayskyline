## Skyline Background
Discussions about developing a rail transit system on Oahu date back to 1968! Over the decades, the project experienced periods of strong support, cancellation, and delays. After being revived in the early 2000s and approved by voters, construction finally began in 2011.

## Skyline Today and Future Expansion
**Segment 1** opened on June 30, 2023, with nine stations connecting Kualakaʻi (East Kapolei) to Hālawa (Aloha Stadium).  

**Segment 2** opened in October 2025, adding four stations and extending service east from Hālawa to Kahauiki (Middle Street Transit Center in Kalihi). Today, Skyline serves 13 stations along approximately 16 miles of track.  

**Segment 3** is under construction and will extend Skyline approximately three miles farther east into Honolulu, adding six stations and ending at Kaʻākaukukui (Civic Center) in Kakaako. Passenger service is anticipated to begin in 2031.  

Beyond Segment 3, no additional extensions have been finalized. However, future expansion possibilities include extending the line to Ala Moana and exploring other potential routes.

## About PraySkyline
Inspired by the faithfulness of the PrayYamanote prayer ministry in Tokyo, we ride the Skyline each week, intentionally seeking God and interceding on behalf of our island home. We pray as the Holy Spirit leads for our leaders, churches, schools, families, the next generation, local businesses, the marginalized  communities and more. We pray for people who ride the train, may we have Holy Spirit sensitivity to see what God sees and cares about.  

May the walls of resistance fall as many come to know Jesus as their Lord and Savior. May His light overcome darkness! May His hope overcome hopelessness! May the name of Jesus be lifted high! May many in Hawaii come to know Jesus as Lord and Savior! Praying for a revival in Hawaii!

## Some general prayer requests as we ride Skyline:

### Pray for the next generation.
Schools, colleges, playgrounds, and families are found throughout the Skyline corridor. Pray that children and young adults would encounter Christians who genuinely love them, experience God’s love through their words and actions, and come to know Jesus.

### Pray for churches to work together.
Pray for unity among churches—Baptist, evangelical, non-denominational, and other Bible-believing churches. Ask God to help them see their communities as a shared mission field and work together to share the gospel and make Jesus known. Pray that these churches would raise up disciples who faithfully follow Jesus and make more disciples. Pray that these churches would be centered on the gospel.

### Pray for the hurting and suffering.
Pray for hope for those who are hurting, friendship for those who are lonely, provision for those who are struggling, and healing for those who are broken. Ask God to surround them with people who will offer compassion, encouragement, and practical help.

### Pray for boldness, love, and compassion.
Pray that Christians would be bold in their faith, loving toward others, compassionate to those in need, and willing to share Jesus with someone today. Ask God to create divine appointments—-conversations on the train, a listening ear for someone who needs to feel heard and seen, a word of encouragement at school or work, or a simple act of kindness that reflects the love of Jesus to a stranger.
