East Kapolei is a growing part of Oʻahu where families are putting down roots, children are growing up, and new opportunities are taking shape. With schools, shopping, recreation, and homes spread throughout the area, daily life brings together longtime local residents and families
starting a new chapter.

## Pray for:
- Families moving into the rapidly growing Kapolei and East Kapolei communities.
- Healthy, Christ-centered families and churches will take root and point people to Jesus.
- Every child would have people around them who love, encourage and protect them.
- Salvation Army Kroc Community Center — a community, recreation, wellness and worship complex. May this be a gathering place for bible studies and other ministry activities. May their dormitory host many visiting mission teams.
- Ka Makana Ali’i and other local businesses — pray for employees, business owners and families experiencing financial hardship
- Churches: Makakilo Bible Church
