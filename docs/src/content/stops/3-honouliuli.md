Hoʻopili is a new and developing residential community where families are settling in, children
are making friends, and neighbors are building community together. Near these new neighborhoods are acres of land that belong to Ho Farms and Aloun Farms.

## Pray for:
- God would place His people throughout the community — schools, businesses,neighborhoods. May they be bold in sharing the gospel, loving and kind in interactions.
- Families, children, schools, teachers and community leaders in the developing Hoʻopili area located between Kapolei and Ewa. Pray for Honouliuli Middle School and the upcoming East Kapolei Elementary School.
- Local farmers and a plentiful harvest.
- Churches: Aloha Nani and Pearlside Kapolei
