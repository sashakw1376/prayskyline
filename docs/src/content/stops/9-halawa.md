Hālawa sits at the crossroads of neighborhoods, recreation, transportation, and Hawaiʻi's sports and entertainment life. The area connects residents from ʻAiea, Salt Lake, Moanalua, and beyond. Aloha Stadium was the site of many concerts, pro bowls, and graduation ceremonies. Close by is Pearl Harbor Memorial, which receives over 1 million visitors every year.

## Pray for:
- Wisdom for our leaders who are planning the new Aloha Stadium Entertainment District. May this development benefit local residents and create opportunities for the community.
- The many people that go to the Swap Meet, including vendors, workers, families and visitor. May God provide for local vendors and create opportunities for meaningful connections.
- Churches in the Halawa, Aiea and Salt Lake areas to be centered on the gospel and to be bold in sharing Jesus throughout their communities.
- Students, teachers, coaches, administrators at Radford High School, Makalapa Elementary — May God protect the students from harmful influences and strengthen those struggling with loneliness and anxiety.
