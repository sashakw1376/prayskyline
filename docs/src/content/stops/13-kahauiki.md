Kalihi is a diverse, working-class community with deep roots in Honolulu. Generations of residents and families from many cultural backgrounds have helped shape its neighborhoods, businesses, schools, and churches. As a major transportation hub, Kalihi brings together people traveling to work, school, appointments, and home.

## Pray for:
- Families, children, schools, businesses, and immigrants throughout Kalihi.
- Churches: Kalihi Union Church
