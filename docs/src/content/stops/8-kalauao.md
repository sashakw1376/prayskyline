The Pearlridge area brings together the neighborhoods of ʻAiea, Waimalu, and Pearl City with a busy center for shopping, services, and everyday activities. Families, students, healthcare workers, and local businesses all contribute to the life of this community.

## Pray for:
- All churches in the Aiea and Pearl City area to remain centered on the gospel and grow in unity and cooperation as they reach their communities for Jesus.
- Local businesses, including Sumida Farms, vendors at the Pearlridge Farmers’ Market, coffee shops, Egan’s, and locally owned stores and restaurants throughout the area. Pray for welcoming places where people can gather for Bible studies, prayer, and fellowship.
- Relationships and partnerships among local businesses to strengthen, provide for their needs, and help them sustain their businesses while continuing to serve and support the community.
- Patients and healthcare workers at Pali Momi Hospital, Mary Savio — pray that those who know you will interact with compassion and care.
- Students, teachers, and administrators at Aiea Elementary, Pearlridge Elementary, Gus Webling Elementary, Waimalu Elementary, Aiea Intermediate, Aiea High School, Our Savior Lutheran, St Elizabeth.
