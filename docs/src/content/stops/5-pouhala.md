Waipahu is a community shaped by generations of local families, plantation-era history, and a rich mix of cultures. Its shops, schools, churches, and neighborhood gathering places reflect the everyday life of people working hard and caring for those around them. Waipahu Transit Center is a major hub that services 8 bus lines. The smokestack of Oʻahu Sugar Company remains as a landmark of plantation history.

## Pray for:
- Provision and wisdom for longtime local businesses along Farrington Highway.
- Students, teachers, and administrators at Waipahu High School and Waipahu Intermediate, that these schools would be safe, healthy environments where students can learn and thrive.
- Filipino Community Center as it serves the practical and social needs of the Filipino community.
- Unity among area churches, that they would be the hands and feet of Jesus, working together to serve their communities and share the gospel beyond the walls of their buildings.
- Churches: West Oahu Christian Church
